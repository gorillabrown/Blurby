## 2. Lessons candidates (engineering discoveries of this run)

| # | Candidate lesson | Evidence |

| L1 | espeak's British English (`en-gb`, Kokoro's `b` voices) is non-rhotic in isolation ("for" → `fɔː`) and pronounces a linking r before a vowel in context ("for a" → `fəɹə`); any identification evidence built from per-word phonemes must allow that `ɹ`, and must count syllables per word rather than on the joined forms (vowel-final + vowel-initial words merge into one run). The first full matrix failed P0-2 on exactly this unit at all 25 rates; the fix changed no table and no span. | `phase0/MAPPING_POLICY.md` §4; journal S1 00:15; `lib/wordmap.mjs` |
| L2 | A generation log's "0 fails" counts exceptions only; a table-level verdict (`reconciled: false`) is visible only in the summary. Gate on the summary's exit code, never on the log. | journal S1 00:15 |
| L3 | Kokoro (`model_q4.onnx`, kokoro-js 1.2.1) renders the leading silence of a chunk at roughly 360–500 ms regardless of speed, while the BOS token's predicted duration scales with 1/speed (575 ms at 0.80x → 225 ms at 2.00x); the table's first word is therefore late by 40–120 ms at ≤ 1.00x and early by 50–150 ms at 2.00x. At interior word onsets after a pause the table is late by about two native frames divided by speed (means +63 ms at 0.80x, +47 at 1.00x, +36 at 1.40x, +9 to +30 at 2.00x; reference uncertainty ≤ 9.5 ms). | `phase0/acoustic/*.json`; VERDICT.md part (ii) |
| L4 | onnxruntime-node's default thread count was the slowest setting on this host for Kokoro (worst warm 4.5 s); 4 intra-op threads was best (2.0 s); 8 oversubscribed (3.1 s). The first generation at a new input shape costs about twice a repeat (3.2–4.1 s vs 1.6–1.8 s for 11 words), so a harness that repeats one text is optimistic for a product whose chunks change shape every time. | `phase0/latency.threads-*.json`; `phase0/latency.json`; VERDICT.md P0-5 |
| L5 | Running the generation matrix while anything else loads the CPU inflated per-chunk generation time by about 40 % (3,654 s under load vs 2,612 s quiet for the same 500 chunks, byte-identical audio); timing fields in tables are only comparable when recorded on a quiet machine. | journal S1 01:10 |
| L6 | Model precision does not move Kokoro's alignment error: the fp32 and fp16 exports, with the same duration-exposing edit, reproduce the q4 error at every paired anchor within −15…+10 ms (mean change under 1 ms) on passage A at 1.00x on both voices; their duration tables differ from q4's by a few frames and the audio moves with them. | L7 | Kokoro's engine component of the 2 s warm budget at 1.00x — 1.4–1.7 s of generation for an 11-word chunk plus a 0.29–0.44 s leading silence — leaves a 3 % margin on a quiet 12-core host at 4 intra-op threads and none under ordinary desktop load; the product's exact levers (trim the leading silence at playback by its known sample count, a shorter first chunk within the token cap, generating the next chunk while the current plays) are what make the end-to-end budget reachable, and a harness that repeats fixed texts cannot exercise them. | VERDICT.md § P0-5 (observations 2–4 and the gate reruns) |

| L10 | transformers.js 3.8.1's local-file path (`FileResponse`, used when `localModelPath` is set and no cache hits) streams the whole file into a `ReadableStream` nothing reads, then reads it again: for Kokoro's 291 MB q4 model that is 4,658 chunks of 64 KB of garbage per worker thread. ## 3. Observations for the roadmap entry (not changes; the planner decides)

- The charter's P0-3 inconclusive trigger ("repaired twice") was reached: the literal pause-k ↔ mark-k association (T4) could not be used as written because Kokoro realizes pauses without marks and skips marks at dense commas; the reference now aligns pauses to marks monotonically on text-only priors and leaves every neighbour of a mismatch unresolved (repair 1), and word offsets before pauses were tried as anchors and found invalid because the pause's frames belong to the preceding phoneme in the table (repair 2, kept as a side report). Passage B's dense punctuation yields fewer than 10 resolved anchors at every rate; 2.00x yields fewer than 10 on both passages.
- P0-5 passes at 4 intra-op threads (worst warm 1.94 s, worst cold 3.03 s over 160 + 20 trials) and fails at onnxruntime-node's default (worst warm 4.5 s); the product must set the thread count explicitly, and the leading silence alone is 0.29–0.44 s of every total. The owner's G1 decision carries the route (continue, precision trial, or bake-off).
