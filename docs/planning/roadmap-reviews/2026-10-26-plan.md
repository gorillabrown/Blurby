## Product choices proposed for owner approval

- **Regular-display Narrate:** preserve the viewport when the active selected word is already visible; align the paused highlight to that word. Follow only when it would leave the reading area or the reader explicitly jumps back. E-ink uses its separate screenwise contract.
- **Page numbers:** stable, clearly marked approximate 250-word pages across modes; do not represent estimates as physical/publisher page numbers. Preserve existing Keep/Move page calculations in the copy-only fix.
- **Enabling E-ink during Focus:** move to Page, paused, at the same canonical location. Hide/guard Focus buttons, shortcuts and remembered-mode restore while E-ink remains on. Do not auto-start another mode.
- **Book typography:** normalize decorative inline sizes/dropcaps to the reader scale while preserving emphasis, links and semantic superscript/subscript. Current publisher small/dropcap sizes are intentionally preserved by the release helper, so flattening them is a design change.
- **Folder switch:** show an explicit loading state for the selected folder; do not present old-folder cards as its contents. Keep cached/persisted records intact while scanning, and show failure/retry if needed. Do not silently rewrite the tested 30-second cancellation contract.
- **Completion confirmation:** an assumed finish on exit offers Keep in Library and Archive; default/dismiss keeps it visible. Archive requires an affirmative action and makes it findable in Archive. Compatible explicit Mark complete/Undo behavior stays to be reconciled in AUTO-ARCHIVE-1's amended specification.
