## Key design decision encoded

The desync fix is a **bounded rebuild behind the existing clean seam**, not a from-scratch rewrite: one lag-compensated heard cursor (sourced from the scheduler's already-compensated `getAudioProgress()` position) drives both the highlight and `FlowScrollEngine.followWord`, with a tunable `TTS_VISUAL_CURSOR_LAG_MS`. Kokoro chunking/playback and the anchor word — the parts Evan confirmed work — are explicitly preserved.
