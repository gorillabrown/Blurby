Chapter One: The Small Garden

At dawn, a gardener opened the wooden gate and placed a blue watering can beside the path. The morning air was cool. A robin watched from the fence while the gardener checked the young plants one row at a time. Each plant received a little water, and each row received a careful look.

The garden had four beds. The first held carrots, the second held beans, the third held herbs, and the fourth held flowers. A narrow path connected the beds to a bench beneath an apple tree. Nothing in this small garden moved quickly. Even the watering can seemed to wait patiently between its journeys.

Chapter Two: A Quiet Walk

After the work was done, the gardener walked along the river. Sunlight reached the water through the leaves. A small boat rested at the bank, tied with a rope to an old post. Beyond the bridge, a baker was opening a shop, and the smell of warm bread drifted across the street.

The gardener stopped to read a notice beside the bridge. It described a gathering at the town library on Saturday. People would bring books, share a short passage, and talk about places they had visited. The gardener decided to bring a book about trees, with a paper marker placed at the beginning of a favorite chapter.

Chapter Three: The Library Table

On Saturday, the library windows stood open. Four chairs surrounded a round table, and a glass jug of water stood beside a stack of clean cups. The first reader spoke slowly, leaving time between sentences. The next reader chose a poem about rain. Everyone listened until the final word had settled into the quiet room.

When the gathering ended, the gardener returned home by the river path. The plants needed no more water that evening. The bench beneath the apple tree was dry, the gate was closed, and the blue watering can remained beside the path. A robin returned to the fence and watched the garden in the fading light.
